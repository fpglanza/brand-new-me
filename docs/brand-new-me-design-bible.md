# Brand New Me Design Bible

## 1. Product Philosophy

Brand New Me is not a habit tracker.

It is a personal RPG about resisting entropy. The app should make daily self-care, physical training, creative work, and environment maintenance feel like part of a coherent heroic identity rather than a checklist of obligations.

The tone should be hopeful, sustainable, and psychologically healthy. The product should reward consistency without creating shame. Missed days should feel recoverable. A new day is a fresh run. A new week is a fresh challenge.

The core design values are:

- Simplicity over system sprawl.
- Consistency over intensity.
- Sustainability over perfection.
- Closure without shame.
- Progress that feels personal, not performative.

## 2. Core Pillars

### Hero

The Hero represents permanent identity growth. This is who the player is becoming over time.

Hero systems should feel long-term, personal, and cumulative. They should not reset daily or weekly.

### Dark Empress

The Dark Empress represents weekly entropy pressure. She is not a monster to defeat. She is a powerful presence to appease by maintaining enough order and consistency.

The Weekly Challenge should create closure, not shame.

### Kingdom

The Kingdom represents the player's environment: home, chores, domestic order, and basic stewardship.

The Kingdom is separate from Hero identity. It should not become a Hero stat and should not award Hero XP by default.

## 3. Implemented Systems

### Hero XP

Hero XP is permanent. It never resets daily or weekly.

Quest completion awards Hero XP. Unchecking a completed quest removes the awarded XP. XP persists in SQLite.

### Level Titles

Hero level titles currently map as:

- Levels 1-4: Novice Adventurer
- Levels 5-9: Disciplined Wanderer
- Levels 10-14: Shadow Hunter
- Levels 15-19: Kingdom Builder
- Level 20+: Entropy Breaker

Titles are identity flavor only. They do not change mechanics.

### Hero Attributes

Permanent Hero Attributes currently exist:

- Body
- Mind
- Purpose

They persist forever and represent identity growth.

Attribute gains are weighted by quest type. Unchecking a quest removes the same attribute points, never below zero.

Hero Attribute bars use milestone scaling rather than relative-to-highest-stat scaling.

### Daily Progress

Daily Progress is separate from Hero XP.

It is calculated only from today's completed quests. It resets daily, targets 100, and can exceed 100.

Daily Progress meaning:

- Below 80: Keep pushing back entropy.
- 80-99: Good enough. Consistency held.
- 100-149: Successful day. Order restored.
- 150+: Excellent day. Entropy routed.

### Quests

Daily quests are generated by local date.

The current quest system includes:

- Baseline daily quests.
- Weekday-specific training and stewardship quests.
- One-day workout carryover.
- Today-only display.
- SQLite persistence.
- Completion and uncompletion.

### Bonus Effort

Bonus Effort allows the player to add meaningful completed activities that were not scheduled today.

Bonus Effort:

- Creates a quest row for today.
- Marks it completed immediately.
- Awards Hero XP.
- Counts toward Daily Progress.
- Affects Dark Empress Demand through existing Shadow mechanics.
- Uses the same Hero Attribute mapping as scheduled quests.
- Can be unchecked like other quests.

### Weekly Challenge

Weekly Challenge rewards consistency, not raw quest volume.

Victory Day:

- Daily Progress >= 80

Weekly result:

- 0-3 Victory Days: Defeat
- 4 Victory Days: Draw
- 5-7 Victory Days: Victory

Strong Days and Legendary Days are tracked for flavor:

- Strong Day: Daily Progress 100-149
- Legendary Day: Daily Progress 150+

They do not affect the weekly result.

### Dark Empress Demand

The Dark Empress uses the existing Shadow power mechanic but is presented narratively as Demand.

Demand starts high at the beginning of the week and decreases as the player completes quests.

The Dark Empress is not defeated. She is appeased.

### Protect the Kingdom Checklist

Protect the Kingdom is a separate stewardship checklist layer.

It includes cadence-based checklist items:

- Weekly
- Every 2 Weeks
- Every 3 Weeks
- Monthly

Checklist completion persists by cadence window. Items reset only when their cadence window expires.

Protect the Kingdom does not affect:

- Hero XP
- Daily Progress
- Hero Attributes
- Dark Empress Demand
- Weekly Challenge

### DEV DEBUG Tools

DEV DEBUG exists for testing persistence, daily rollover, weekly rollover, quest reseeding, shadow demand, finalized results, and Kingdom checklist behavior.

It is not a product feature and should remain clearly marked as development-only.

## 4. Hero Design

Hero XP is permanent.

Body, Mind, and Purpose are permanent.

Fuel is not permanent.

Recovery is not permanent.

Stewardship is not a Hero stat.

The Hero represents who the player is becoming. Hero identity should be the first thing the player feels on Home.

Hero progression should feel earned but not complicated. Avoid adding equipment, inventory, skill trees, or stat formulas until there is a clear gameplay reason.

## 5. Dark Empress Design

The Dark Empress is not defeated.

She is appeased.

She represents pressure, standards, judgment, and entropy. Her presence should feel powerful, dangerous, amused, and regal.

The Weekly Challenge measures consistency.

Victory Day:

- Daily Progress >= 80

Weekly result:

- 0-3 Victory Days: Defeat
- 4 Victory Days: Draw
- 5-7 Victory Days: Victory

Result flavor:

- Victory: The Dark Empress is pleased. Order was maintained this week.
- Draw: The Dark Empress watches silently. More will be required next week.
- Defeat: The Dark Empress is displeased. Entropy spreads through the kingdom.

## 6. Kingdom Design

The Kingdom represents the player's environment.

The Kingdom should not use Hero XP.

The Kingdom should not be a Hero stat.

The Kingdom can be slightly punishing because real environments decay. This is different from the Hero, which should feel identity-building and forgiving.

Planned Kingdom model:

- Prosperity: current state of the Kingdom. Can rise and fall.
- Legacy: lifetime stewardship. Never decreases.

Prosperity should represent whether the environment is currently being maintained.

Legacy should represent the long-term record of stewardship across time.

## 7. Planned Systems

Likely planned systems:

- Kingdom Prosperity and Legacy.
- Fuel as a daily layer.
- Recovery as a weekly layer.
- Weekly Review maybe later.
- Better hero sprite progression.
- Future bosses after the Dark Empress.

Fuel should remain daily and practical. It should not become a permanent Hero stat.

Recovery should likely be weekly or rhythm-based. It should not become a permanent Hero stat.

Future bosses should follow the same principle as the Dark Empress: they should clarify a life pressure, not add complexity for its own sake.

## 8. Rejected / Deferred Ideas

Rejected or deferred for now:

- Loot.
- Equipment.
- Skill trees.
- Achievements.
- Social features.
- Kingdom XP.
- Stewardship as a Hero stat.
- Permanent Fuel stat.
- Permanent Recovery stat.
- Overcomplicated chore tracking.
- Weekly Review for now.

These ideas are not permanently forbidden, but they should not be added unless they clearly improve daily use.

## 9. Development Rules

Codex is for implementation.

Art direction and sprites may need a manual or image-AI workflow.

Do not let Codex over-polish visual assets.

Prefer simple architecture.

Prefer small prompts.

Never add systems unless they clearly improve daily use.

Avoid large speculative abstractions. Refactor when the current code shape starts slowing down implementation or creating real risk.

Keep mechanics deterministic and easy to inspect.

Prefer SQLite persistence for real game progress and local React state for temporary view/UI behavior.

## 10. Current MVP Status

Brand New Me currently has a working RPG hub flow with persistent Hero XP, daily quests, Bonus Effort, permanent Hero Attributes, Daily Progress, Weekly Challenge, Dark Empress Demand, Protect the Kingdom checklist, and DEV DEBUG tooling.

The app has moved beyond a checklist prototype and now has three clear product layers:

- Hero: permanent identity growth.
- Dark Empress: weekly consistency pressure.
- Kingdom: environment stewardship.

The likely next coding milestone is Kingdom Prosperity / Legacy.

That milestone would give Protect the Kingdom a meaningful progression model while preserving the separation between Hero identity and environment maintenance.
